\subsection{Closed-Loop Flight Trajectory Analysis}
The flight dynamics and actuation commands synthesized by the converged PPO agent during a representative descent trajectory are illustrated across six synchronized time-history channels in \Cref{fig:trajectory_overview}. The learned control law executes a three-phase optimal landing strategy:

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.92\linewidth]{figures/trajectory_analysis.pdf}
    \caption{Multi-channel trajectory profile of the converged closed-loop PPO controller during nominal descent, illustrating altitude, horizontal displacement, pitch regulation, mass depletion, and continuous throttle commands.}
    \label{fig:trajectory_overview}
\end{figure}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Phase I: Attitude Capture and Cross-Track Steering ($t \in [0.0, 2.2]\,\si{s}$):} Dispatched from high altitude with stochastic initial velocity and attitude disturbances ($\theta_0 \sim \mathcal{U}(-0.3, 0.3)\,\si{rad}$), the controller immediately engages differential RCS pulsing ($|u_{\text{side}}| \approx 0.60$) to arrest angular momentum ($\omega \to 0$). Simultaneously, the agent banks into a controlled tilt angle ($\theta \approx 0.15\,\si{rad}$), directing a component of main engine thrust to nullify cross-track horizontal velocity ($v_x \to 0$).
    \item \textbf{Phase II: Mass-Adaptive Gravity-Turn Deceleration ($t \in [2.2, 5.8]\,\si{s}$):} As the vehicle approaches the vertical axis of the landing pad, the controller re-aligns the fuselage upright ($\theta \to 0$) and increases main throttle ($u_{\text{main}} \approx 0.70$). Critically, as propellant burns at $0.40\,\text{units/step}$ and the structural mass declines dynamically from $m_{\text{wet}} \approx 4.82\,\si{kg}$ toward dry mass, the physical control authority $\vec{F}_{\text{main}}/m(t)$ increases. Demonstrating deep closed-loop adaptability, the Actor network dynamically throttles back main engine output from $0.78$ down to $0.58$, maintaining a smooth, constant descent deceleration without inducing vertical limit cycles or overshoot.
    \item \textbf{Phase III: Terminal Touchdown and Quiescent Settling ($t \in [5.8, 7.6]\,\si{s}$):} In the terminal $1.5\,\si{m}$ descent bracket, the agent executes gentle throttle pulses to establish a steady descent velocity $v_y \approx -0.10\,\si{m/s}$. Both articulated cantilever legs make simultaneous contact with the terrain; the kinetic energy is absorbed by the revolute spring-damper joints, and the lander settles safely to rest in the sleep state.
\end{enumerate}
